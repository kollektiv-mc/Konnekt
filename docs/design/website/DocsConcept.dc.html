How remote access works

Konnekt stays on your computer. Remote access lets an approved browser, or Claude, reach it, through four layers you switch on one at a time.

DURATION~4 min
TOPICSRemote access · Security
01

The path a request takes

Phone or laptopa browser you approved
Cloudflare tunnelquick, or your own address
Your computerKonnekt checks and serves
Your serversconsole, files, RCON

Nothing is hosted.
The tunnel only forwards; everything else happens on your computer.

02

Four layers

01ListenerOff until you switch it on, and off again after 30 idle minutes.
02TunnelOptional. Reaches the listener from outside, through Cloudflare.
03PasswordEvery browser signs in with it. Changing it signs all out.
04ApprovalNew browsers and Claude clients wait for your approval.
